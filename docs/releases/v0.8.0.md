# v0.8.0 — Sound & Fury

The game has a voice. This release lights up the previously-silent audio scaffold
with a full set of sound effects and a looping music track, plus in-game volume
controls for Music and SFX.

## Highlights

- **Sound effects everywhere.** Firing, enemy fire, kills, detonations, taking
  damage, star pickups, level-ups, and game over all have sounds — every event a
  player perceives as discrete.
- **Looping background music** plays under every scene (Mr.Cat's bit-piano track).
- **Independent Music & SFX volume controls**, adjustable from the main menu *and*
  mid-run from the pause overlay. Stepped −/+ buttons in 10% increments; 0% is off.
- Settings **persist** across sessions (localStorage), defaulting to Music 30% /
  SFX 50%.

## Details

- **Variant pools** — explosions draw at random from three takes so repeated kills
  don't replay one grating sample.
- **Role-split samples** — the player's gun and enemy fire use distinct laser
  sounds; player damage and the per-bullet impact spark use distinct hit sounds, so
  "I got hit" never sounds like "I hit them."
- **Throttled impact** — the per-bullet Hit Spark sound is rate-limited (and quiet)
  so heavy Multishot/Rapid-Fire builds don't turn it into noise.
- **Autoplay unlock** — music reliably starts on the player's first interaction,
  working around browser autoplay blocking.
