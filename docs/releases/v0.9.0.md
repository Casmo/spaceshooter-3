# v0.9.0 — Debris & the Fortress

Kills leave a mark, and the late game gets a fortress. This release adds a
cosmetic debris shower to every "clean" enemy death and introduces the
SpaceStation — a slow side-raking wall of a foe for the deep waves.

## Highlights

- **Debris on kills.** Destroying a Swarmer, Gunner, Warden, Station, Mini-boss,
  or Boss now flings out a shower of ship fragments that scatter, tumble, drift
  to rest, and pop into small explosions. Purely visual — no damage, no sound.
- **New enemy: the SpaceStation.** A heavy, slow-descending fortress (wave 20+)
  that lingers in your zone and rakes a vertical comb of pure-horizontal bullets
  out both flanks on a telegraphed cadence. Positional area-denial: clear its
  vertical band or thread the comb's gaps.

## Details

### Debris

- **Count scales with size** — the number of chunks derives from the dead
  enemy's sprite scale, so a small Swarmer sheds one or two and a Boss sheds a
  full handful (up to five).
- **Six fragment shapes**, cut from the shared Debris atlas, picked at random and
  drawn at roughly half the source enemy's scale.
- **Ease-out drift** — each chunk launches in a random direction, decelerates to
  a near-stop while tumbling, and fades slightly (to 50%) before its end-of-life
  pop reuses the standard explosion burst at half size.
- **Deliberately excluded** — the Mine and Bomber (they detonate their own
  blast), Asteroids (they split into smaller Asteroids), and shot-off Shield
  Nodes. Debris is reserved for whole-enemy "clean" kills.

### SpaceStation

- **Side-raking comb** — fires pure-horizontal bullets out both flanks: 5 per
  side at wave 20, ramping to 10 by wave 35.
- **Telegraphed cadence** — the rake is signposted before it fires, so the
  threat is readable positioning, not twitch dodging.
- Spawns at an 8% budget share from wave 20 onward.

## Under the hood

- New `DebrisPool` (pooled, non-interactive chunks that delegate their pop to the
  existing effects pool); all debris tuning lives in the `DEBRIS` config block.
  Vocabulary (Debris / Debris chunk) is recorded in `CONTEXT.md`.
- The SpaceStation's pure-horizontal design — and the rejected downward-fan and
  hover-camp alternatives — is recorded in
  [ADR-0015](../adr/0015-spacestation-side-rake-fortress.md).
