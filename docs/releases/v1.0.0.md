# v1.0.0 — Relentless

Spaceshooter 3 reaches 1.0. This release makes the deep endgame relentless and
marks the game feature-complete for its browser debut.

## Highlights

- **The relentless endgame.** From **wave 25 on, every wave carries a mini-boss** —
  not just the every-fifth ones. There is now always a high-HP capstone on the
  field once you're deep in a run.
- **Double trouble on boss waves.** The every-tenth boss waves (30, 40, 50…) now
  field the **Boss *and* a mini-boss** together, instead of the Boss alone.
- **1.0 milestone.** Version bumped to 1.0.0; the game is feature-complete for
  release.

## Details

### The relentless endgame

- **Every wave from 25 gets a mini-boss**, spliced into the normal enemy budget
  mid-wave — so it's a complication on top of a live wave, not a lull.
- **Boss waves stack both capstones.** The Boss descends, then a mini-boss joins
  it; the two are spread across the wave so they don't arrive on the same tick.
- **Kept killable — no soft-lock.** A mini-boss never flees, so a wave only clears
  when it dies. To keep that fair at depth, the per-wave mini-bosses scale on the
  wave's HP multiplier alone (no compounding per-appearance bonus): about
  3,850 HP at wave 25, rising to ~7,450 by wave 50 — tanky but always beatable by
  a wave-25-caliber build. The classic milestone mini-bosses (waves 5, 15) are
  unchanged.
- **A guaranteed Star every wave from 25** (two on boss waves), a small,
  intentional reward for surviving the escalation.
- Only one filler mini-boss is ever alive at a time — a wave never advances until
  it's cleared — so they never pile up across waves (boss waves excepted, by
  design).

## Under the hood

- The threshold is the single knob `WAVES.miniBossEveryWaveFrom` (= 25); the
  Boss/mini-boss overlap lives in the one `composeWave` method. Per-wave
  mini-bosses spawn via a flat-HP variant so their scaling is decoupled from the
  milestone appearance counter.
- The design and its rejected alternatives (full compounding, capped bonus, a
  flee/timeout guard) are recorded in
  [ADR-0016](../adr/0016-relentless-miniboss-endgame.md); the Mini-boss and Wave
  vocabulary is updated in `CONTEXT.md`.
