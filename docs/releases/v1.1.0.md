# v1.1.0 — The Missile

The first post-1.0 release adds the game's **second weapon** — a self-firing
Missile Launcher that rains area-of-effect blasts — and makes every clean kill
hit harder on screen with the new Kill Burst.

## Highlights

- **Missiles — a whole new weapon.** A new epic Upgrade unlocks the **Missile
  Launcher**: while you hold fire it auto-launches one missile per second, on its
  own clock, independent of the gun. Each missile flies straight up and
  **detonates on contact for an area blast** that hits every enemy nearby.
  Damage climbs with every pick, forever.
- **Punchier kills.** Every clean enemy death now flashes a central **Kill
  Burst** on top of the debris shower — the moment of destruction reads as a real
  explosion, not just a puff and a sound.

## Details

### The Missile Launcher

- **A second weapon, not a bullet tweak.** Unlike the six bullet Modifiers
  (Multishot, Pierce, Homing, Explosive, Burn, Bounce) that ride the main gun's
  shots, the Missile Launcher is its **own weapon**. No Modifier touches it, and
  its cadence is fixed — Fire Rate does not speed it up.
- **Trigger-gated, one per second.** It fires only while the fire button is held
  (never idle-fires), launching one Missile every second. The first pick unlocks
  it; every later pick adds Missile damage (unlimited, epic).
- **Launch and climb.** A Missile leaves the nose slow (120 px/s) and
  accelerates to just above bullet speed (1500 px/s) by the time it has climbed a
  third of the screen, then holds. It flies straight up, does not home, and
  silently leaves the field if it hits nothing.
- **The blast.** On contact with any enemy it detonates for a **fixed 110 px
  area blast**, dealing its damage to every enemy body caught in the radius —
  including the one it struck. It never harms the player (the mirror image of the
  enemy Mine and Bomber, whose blasts hit *you*).
- **Wardens hold the line.** A Warden's orbiting Shield **blocks Missiles**: a
  Missile detonates on the first live Shield Node in its path. The Node orbit was
  widened (80 → 120 px) so a blocked blast lands just short of the body — the
  Shield genuinely protects the Warden until a rotating gap lines up and a Missile
  slips through to the hull. (A side effect: the wider ring spreads the gun's
  target gaps a touch, too.)

### Kill Burst

- A clean enemy death (Swarmer, Gunner, Warden, Station, Mini-boss, Boss) now
  plays a single central explosion at the death point, layered on top of the
  existing Debris fragments and sized to the dead ship's footprint. It is purely
  cosmetic — no damage, no extra sound. Explosive enemies (Mine, Bomber) keep
  their own detonation, and Asteroids keep splitting.

## Under the hood

- The Missile reuses the generic projectile pool (with new rotation support for
  the 180° art flip, and origin tracking for the distance-based speed ramp) and
  the Explosion04 burst sheet under a dedicated `missileExplosion` alias. All feel
  values live in the `MISSILE` config block.
- The modifier-vs-weapon decision and the Warden-Shield geometry are recorded in
  [ADR-0018](../adr/0018-missile-launcher-independent-weapon.md); the Kill Burst
  in [ADR-0017](../adr/0017-kill-burst-on-clean-kills.md). `CONTEXT.md` re-scopes
  the old "there is only one weapon" invariant — the gun is still the only *gun*,
  but the Missile Launcher is a second, non-gun weapon.
- Also included: a full [v1.0 dev log](../../devlog-v1.md) tracing the build
  version-by-version.
