# v1.2.0 — The Drones

This release adds the game's **third weapon** — a squadron of orbiting **Drones**
that follow your ship and melt whatever they lock onto with a laser that only gets
hotter the longer it burns.

## Highlights

- **Drones — an orbiting laser escort.** A new **legendary** Upgrade grants a
  small drone that floats around your ship and, entirely on its own, fires a
  **continuous beam** at the nearest enemy. The beam **starts weak and ramps up
  the longer it holds a target, with no cap** — so it shreds tanky enemies and
  keeps scaling straight into the endgame. Each pick adds another drone, up to
  **three**.
- **A weapon that fights while you dodge.** Unlike the Missile Launcher, the
  Drones need no trigger — they defend you even when you're not shooting, watching
  a different arc each and fanning their beams across the field.

## Details

### The Drones

- **Third independent weapon.** Like the Missile Launcher, the Drones are their
  own weapon — no bullet Modifier touches them, and Fire Rate is irrelevant. They
  are **indestructible**: nothing can shoot them down.
- **Orbit and float.** The drones ride a slowly-rotating ring around your ship and
  *soft-follow* it, lagging and swinging to catch up as you dart around rather than
  welding rigidly to your side.
- **Per-drone targeting.** Each drone independently locks the **nearest enemy
  within range of its own position** — so with three drones out, they naturally
  spread across three threats (coverage), while a lone boss gets all three beams
  at once (concentration).
- **The ramping beam.** A fresh lock is gentle. Damage then climbs **linearly and
  without limit** for as long as the beam holds that same target — and **resets to
  the start the instant the target dies or leaves range**, at which point the drone
  re-acquires and spins up again. The result is a **tank-melter**: fierce on a lone
  high-HP enemy, mild against a fast-dying swarm.
- **It heats up as it kills.** The beam visibly grows — thicker and shifting
  **cyan → white → orange-red** as its damage climbs — so you can read a drone's
  charge at a glance. (The look tops out for readability; the real damage keeps
  rising past it.)
- **Wardens get melted.** The beam **ignores the Warden's Shield** and burns the
  body directly (the same shield-agnostic stance the Missile takes). A legendary
  escort is meant to hard-counter one enemy — chewing through Shield Nodes first is
  noted as a possible future refinement.
- **One drone per level, cap 3.** Legendary rarity (draw weight 1). The Upgrade
  grants *only* an extra drone each level — no hidden stat scaling; three beams is
  the whole power jump.

## Under the hood

- The Drones are a scene-owned `DroneSwarm` — the first friendly entity that
  *follows* the player and the first *continuous beam* in the game. Targeting
  reuses the generation-stamped lock trick from Homing (to tell "target gone" from
  "pool slot reused"); the continuous damage follows the Burn model
  (`hp -= dps · dt` per frame); the beam is a new `Graphics` renderer whose width
  and heat-color are driven by the current damage. All feel values live in the
  `DRONE` config block.
- The design — the per-target uncapped ramp (chosen over a firing-state ramp), the
  shield-ignoring beam, and the follow-the-player entity model — is recorded in
  [ADR-0019](../adr/0019-drone-orbiting-companion-weapon.md). It follows the
  independent-weapon template that [ADR-0018](../adr/0018-missile-launcher-independent-weapon.md)
  explicitly anticipated for "drones, beams." `CONTEXT.md` gains **Drone** and
  **Drone Beam** terms.
