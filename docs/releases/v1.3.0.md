# v1.3.0 — The Lode

This release adds the game's first enemy that is an **opportunity rather than a
threat**: a golden, boss-HP rock that drifts across the top of the field every few
waves, leaking treasure as it goes and bursting open if you can kill it before it
leaves. Every **Star** in the game also gains motion — pickups now drift toward you
instead of sitting where the kill happened.

It also ships the **Drone Heat** rework, which landed after v1.2.0 went out.

## Highlights

- **The Lode — a treasure run on a timer.** From **wave 15**, and every third wave
  after it, a shimmering gold rock drifts across the **top** of the field. It never
  shoots and never comes down at you. It just crosses — and if you can burn through
  its boss-grade HP before it reaches the far side, it bursts into a **shower of
  10+ Stars**. Miss the window and it simply leaves; you keep the handful of Stars
  it dripped on the way past. **No penalty, no failure state** — only loot you
  didn't collect.
- **The interesting decision.** Everything else on the field is trying to kill you.
  The Lode is the first thing worth *ignoring danger for*. Chasing it means turning
  your guns away from the adds still spawning around you — and on wave 30 you might
  be splitting fire between a Boss, a Mini-boss and a Lode at once.
- **Stars now drift.** Every Star in the game sinks gently downward for its life, so
  drops that used to expire untouched high on the field now come to you. The Lode's
  death payout is flung **outward through the explosion** and settles into a cluster
  you can sweep — which is the moment your **Tractor Beam** levels have been waiting
  for.
- **Drones keep their heat.** A Drone Beam's damage no longer resets when its target
  dies. (Shipped after v1.2.0 — see below.)

## Details

### The Lode

- **Where and when.** Waves **15, 18, 21, 24, 27, 30…** — exactly one per Lode wave,
  forever. It arrives **mid-wave**, while adds are still coming, and it appears even
  on waves that already carry a Mini-boss or a Boss, so the deep endgame never
  quietly loses the feature.
- **How it moves.** Enters from the **left or right at random**, at a slightly
  different altitude each time, and drifts straight across at a constant slow speed
  — about **16 seconds** end to end. That window is **the same at wave 45 as at wave
  15**: it never speeds up as the run escalates, so what you learn about it stays
  true.
- **Boss-grade HP.** It scales with the wave but **never compounds per appearance**,
  so it stays a real check without ever becoming impossible to cash in. An **HP Bar**
  appears the moment you damage it, so you can judge mid-crossing whether you're
  going to make it.
- **It never shoots.** The tension comes entirely from what you're neglecting while
  you shoot at it, not from a new attack pattern.
- **But flying into it hurts.** It deals Mini-boss-grade contact damage — and
  **survives the collision**. There is no ram shortcut; out-damaging it is the only
  way to open it.
- **The drip.** It leaks a Star every ~1.6s as it crosses, so even a failed attempt
  pays you something for the damage you dealt. Those Stars sink out of the high lane
  toward your zone on their own.
- **The burst.** Killing it pays **10 Stars at wave 15, and one more per cadence
  step** — 15 at wave 30, 20 at wave 45, uncapped — plus Boss-parity XP and Score.
  The Stars fly out in every direction through the explosion, slow down, and settle.
- **A valid target for everything.** Homing, Explosive, Burn, Bounce and Missiles all
  land on it. Note that **Drones can't reach it** from your usual position — the top
  lane is outside their range — so the Lode is primarily a gun-and-Missile check.

### Stars

- **Every Star sinks** (~70 px/s, so ~350px over its 5-second life). Pickups read as
  objects in a moving world rather than decals pinned to the screen, and fewer kill
  drops expire untouched.
- **A Star can be launched** with an outward impulse that decays into that sink —
  which is what makes the Lode's payout burst open rather than quietly appear.
- **Nothing else changed.** Star XP, lifetime, drop chance and collect radius are
  untouched, and the magnet still works exactly as before once a Star is in range.
  There is still only **one kind of Star**.

### Drone Heat (landed after v1.2.0)

v1.2.0 shipped Drones whose beam damage **reset every time the target died** — which
made a legendary Upgrade nearly worthless on any wave with more than one enemy, and
read as a bug when the beam snapped from orange-red back to thin cyan.

- Beam damage is now **Heat**, owned by the drone rather than by its lock. It builds
  the whole time the beam fires, **straight through kills and target switches**, with
  no ceiling.
- It falls only while the drone is **idle**, with nothing at all in range — and the
  cooling is *proportional*, so a scorching drone bleeds down fast while the ~3s
  Breather between waves takes the edge off any of them.
- Heat **survives a lost life** and is **frozen** whenever the game is (an Upgrade
  Prompt, a pause, an alt-tab) — deliberating over a card never costs you spin-up.
- The **drone itself is now tinted** by its heat, firing or not, so you can see that
  an idle drone is still scorching and watch its cooling clock run.

This makes a Drone a **sustained-fire** weapon: feeble the moment you pick it up, and
terrifying once it has burned through a wave without a break.

## Under the hood

- The Lode is a new enemy kind in the existing pool, reusing **frame 1 of the
  Asteroids sheet** — unclaimed by the three Asteroid sizes, so its silhouette is its
  own. **No new art or audio ships with this release.** All of its tuning lives in one
  `LODE` config block, and `LODE.startWave` / `LODE.everyWaves` move the whole event.
- Pooled enemies now remember a **base tint**, so a Burn wearing off restores the
  enemy's own colour instead of resetting it to white — without which the Lode's gold
  would vanish the first time you burned it.
- The design is recorded in
  [ADR-0021](../adr/0021-lode-fleeing-high-value-target.md) — the fleeing
  high-value-target archetype, boss HP without per-appearance compounding, the fixed
  window that ignores the wave speed multiplier, splicing *on top of* the wave budget
  rather than consuming a slot, and unrammability — and
  [ADR-0022](../adr/0022-stars-sink-and-can-be-launched.md), which explains why every
  Star in the game gained motion instead of adding a second pickup type. The Drone
  change is [ADR-0020](../adr/0020-drone-heat-persists-and-cools.md).
- **The project gets its first tests.** Vitest arrives with one spec file covering
  wave composition — the Lode's cadence, the exactly-one-per-wave guarantee, that it
  is added on top of the budget rather than consuming an enemy slot, and that it still
  appears alongside a Mini-boss or Boss. `npm test` runs them. The physical and visual
  behaviour stays verified by playtest, as it has been for every enemy so far.

## Known tuning notes

The Lode was **halved in size and speed** after its first playtest (a 160px rock over
16 seconds, down from 320px over 8). Three things follow, and all are single knobs:

- It is **no longer the biggest thing on the field** — it now sits between an Asteroid
  and a SpaceStation, so the **gold shimmer, not the silhouette, carries the
  telegraph**. Worth watching whether a Lode can slip past unnoticed on a busy wave.
- The damage check it asks for **halved** (~335 sustained DPS at wave 15). Early Lodes
  should now be genuinely killable rather than usually escaping.
- The drip roughly **doubled** to ~9 Stars per crossing, since the window doubled at
  the same cadence.
